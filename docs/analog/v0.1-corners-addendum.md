# Analog emulation v0.1, corners addendum, 2026-10-24

Epic #10486, success criterion SC-003. English, ASCII only. This addendum does not change `v0.1-release-notes.md`; it adds what the release notes left open, the board receipts of the four corners other than tt.

## 1. What this adds

The M3d top `specs/fpga/psp103_inv_ax7203.t27` had a board receipt for the tt corner only (`board-psp103-inv-2026-10-20.md`: 1217 of 1217 lines equal to the spec). The ss, ff, fs and sf tables exist and agree with ngspice (#11123, #11214); this addendum puts each of them on the ALINX AX7203 through the same path that produced the tt receipt.

The corner is chosen by one table choice in one spec, not by a fork: `BOARD_CORNER` in `specs/analog/mos_psp103_data.t27` (TT = 0, FF = 1, SS = 2, FS = 3, SF = 4) selects the tables and the run that the clocked port `on_clock` reads. `t27c gen-verilog` writes it as `parameter [31:0] BOARD_CORNER = N;`; the `data.v` files of two corners differ in that line and nowhere else (checked between ss and ff: one line of 99959). The committed value is 0 (tt). A corner build sets that one literal for the generation only.

New in the specs: the four runs `SS_RUN`, `FF_RUN`, `FS_RUN`, `SF_RUN` (400 samples each) with their pins `PIN_<c>_RUN`; the ROM port `rom_tn`, `rom_tp`, `rom_qn`, `rom_qp`, `rom_ex` that picks the corner; a data-spec test that reads all five corners through that port; and a board-spec test that runs the machine on each corner's tables and checks every sample and the fold against `EXP_S_<c>_Q`.

## 2. The five corners on the board

The pin is the fold S of the run at N = 18F, the corner's fingerprint. In every Build A capture the board's S at N = 18F equals the pin that `mos_psp103.t27` stated before any board run.

| corner | pin (`EXP_S_<c>_Q`) | Build A: lines with Y equal to the spec's sample | Build A: S at N = 18F | Build B: PASS on every line |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| tt | `0x215DC5EAC4C48576` | 1217 of 1217 (the tt receipt, PASS) | equal | the tt receipt itself (#11220) |
| ss | `0x987F22AC4132345B` | 1226 of 1226 | equal | not run, section 4 |
| ff | `0x836220E2C48AB136` | 1220 of 1220 | equal | not run, section 4 |
| fs | `0xA45CC48E565AD2EB` | 1225 of 1225 | equal | not run, section 4 |
| sf | `0x67F02DF65E28F63E` | 1221 of 1221 | equal | not run, section 4 |

Build A loads the corner's tables with the tt samples still in the RAM, so the board's verdict is the prediction (ss 77 PASS and 323 FAIL a round, ff 82 and 318, fs 82 and 318, sf 79 and 321) and the Y column carries the corner's own sample; it is the form of the ss control in the tt note. Build B loads the corner's tables and the corner's own samples, so the board's own verdict is PASS on every line; it is the form of the tt receipt. The board notes are `board-psp103-inv-ss-2026-10-24.md`, `-ff-`, `-fs-` and `-sf-`.

In every Build A capture: the well-formed lines are numbered L = 0 to N-1 with no gap, three complete rounds of 400 are byte-equal (Y, E, S) to each other, and the lines of the partial round after them equal the head of the first round.

## 3. Resources and the post-route clock

From the yosys `stat` and the nextpnr report of each build, not from memory. All four use 1219 FF (FDRE 1196, FDSE 23), 0 DSP48E1, 4 RAMB36E1 and 1 RAMB18E1 (the four 625-word tables and the 400-word run).

| corner | LUT (yosys, LUT2..LUT6) | INV | CARRY4 | nextpnr SLICE_LUTX | seed | Fmax before / after routing |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| tt (2026-10-20) | 1426 | 170 | 207 | 2768 | default | 72.73 / 103.61 MHz |
| ss | 1414 | 170 | 207 | 2756 | default | 77.05 / 108.85 MHz |
| ff | 1420 | 170 | 207 | 2757 | default | 77.23 / 108.26 MHz |
| fs | 1403 | 173 | 208 | 2758 | 2 | 77.37 / 105.98 MHz (default seed: 76.42 / 88.83, FAIL at 100) |
| sf | 1411 | 170 | 207 | 2748 | 2 | 77.57 / 107.17 MHz (default seed: 73.14 / 95.93, FAIL at 100) |

The post-route clock is nextpnr's estimate for one placement. It is not a speed claim and not a property of the netlist: fs and sf needed a second seed to pass 100 MHz in this run.

## 4. What is not done

Build B, the board receipt in the form of the tt one, has not been run for any of the four corners. The build, the load and the judge go through the T27 OS pane, and its server (`~/.t27os/pty.sock`) stopped answering at 21:50 on 2026-10-24: the socket file was re-created and nothing listens on it, while the old server process and the app are alive. The request to the holder of the pane, with the options and the default, was sent at 21:59. Until a Build B capture exists, no corner is claimed as "PASS on the board" and SC-003 is not claimed complete: the Build A receipts above are the evidence that the four corners' tables run on the board and give the spec's samples.

## 5. Not claimed

No speed claim and no power claim. One gate waveform (two levels, an ideal step) and one load (500 fF with dt = 100 ps). Agreement of the board with the spec's fixed-point run, not with ngspice; the spec's distance to ngspice is the one `mos_psp103.t27` and the tables PRs measure.
