# t27 analog emulation v0.1 -- release notes, DRAFT 2026-10-20

Status: a draft for M6 (target 2026-11-16, epic #10486). Every number has its source next to it (a PR, a test or a receipt); a number without a source is not here. Nothing below is a speed claim.

## What v0.1 is

An analog circuit given as a SPICE netlist with IHP SG13G2 (PSP103) device models becomes `.t27` specs that are at once the software reference and the FPGA design. The specs live in `specs/analog/`, the netlist front end in `specs/tools/t27o/`, the checks run with `t27c test-report <spec>` and against ngspice-47 with the OSDI models.

## What it does not claim

- No speed claim: ngspice's wall time is mostly process start-up and no build time of the board design is measured against it.
- The two board tops are behavioural: they do not read the PSP103 tables, so the FPGA size of a PSP103 solver is not measured.
- M4e is not predicted end to end: scenario S5 (the late decisions of the converter) is not met, three of four late-decision offsets are within 0.2 mV and one misses by 0.29 mV (#11124).
- Weak inversion is met for the tt corner only; the other four corners have the 25 x 25 current tables of M3b and no log tables.
- The comparator and the switch of the whole converter are not yet at PSP103 level inside the converter loop.
- The full demo of the external team's VACASK example does not run on the VACASK obtained; only the comparator and the DAC were compared (#11133).
- The external team's per-block self-checking checks are not applied yet; nothing here says the converter passes them.
- This is a SAR ADC and its blocks, not a full mixed-signal chip.

## Reproduce

`t27c test-report specs/analog/<spec>.t27` for a spec's tests (vacuous passes must be 0); the decks under `specs/analog/decks/` run with ngspice-47 and the IHP-Open-PDK at the commit named in each table; the board receipts are in `docs/analog/board-regression-2026-10-19.md`. The night report of 2026-10-19 (`docs/analog/night-report-2026-10-19.md`) has the sources of each number.
