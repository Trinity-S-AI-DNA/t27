## Every speed-up as one word (#8952)

- `tri hot watch` runs `t27c frontier --watch`: a save reruns that spec and its importers (#8751, #8825, #8856).
- `tri hot frontier` runs `t27c frontier --list`: what a build may reuse, ~1 s warm (#8741, #8847).
- `tri hot prebuild <spec> --top <v> [--pnr-seed N]` runs `t27c silicon ... --skip-hardware`: the bitstream goes
  into the reuse cache before a board run, and the cable is never touched (#8797, #8808, #8814).
- `tri hot suite` runs `t27c suite --repo-root . --fast --corpus-only`, every per-spec phase in parallel
  (#8837, #8840, #8845). It passes the suite's exit code through: 1 while the corpus has failures.
- `tri hot caches` lists each cache under `~/.cache/t27` with its entries, its size and its eviction limit, the
  limits read from the generated code of gen_hash_cache.t27 and bitstream_reuse.t27 (#8928, #8933).
- The command strings are constants in specs/tri/hot/hot.t27. Hand-written lines: `cli/tri/src/main.rs` +32 -2,
  `scripts/gen_w371_lean.py` -178.
